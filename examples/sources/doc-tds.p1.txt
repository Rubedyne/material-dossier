Example Ingredients Ltd.                                  Technical Data Sheet
EXAESTER GC                                               Revision 4 / 2025-03-10
Product code: EX-4410

INCI name: Caprylic/Capric Triglyceride
CAS No.: 73398-61-5          EC No.: 277-452-2

Description
Exaester GC is a clear, colourless, odourless oil of medium polarity.

Specification
Appearance (20 °C)             clear, colourless liquid
Acid value                     max. 0.1 mg KOH/g
Saponification value           325 - 345 mg KOH/g
Water content                  max. 0,1 %

Typical data (not part of the specification)
Density (20 °C)                0,945 g/cm³
Viscosity (20 °C)              25 - 33 mPa·s
Spreading value                approx. 550 mm²/10 min
