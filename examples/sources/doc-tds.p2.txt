Application
Recommended use level: 1 - 20 %. Exaester GC is added to the oil phase.
It is stable to oxidation and can be heated up to 80 °C during processing.

Figure 1: Spreading value (mm²/10 min) of Exaester GC compared with two
reference emollients.

Storage and shelf life
Store in the unopened original container, protected from light.
Shelf life: 24 months from the date of manufacture.
